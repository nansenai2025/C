**How solid the research is.** Most of these accounts are unnamed or small, and their Instagram, TikTok and Facebook content can't be read from the web. Only one account, @vaibhavsisinty, has public data on how it makes money. Everything else below is inferred from what each account posts about (the "Întrebarea asociată" column). Treat the patterns as strong signals, not verified numbers.

**One assumption.** I treat Human2OS as your own framework or brand. That's based on how README.md uses it: your LinkedIn banner and Featured link point to it, and you compare it with an ADHD skill for Claude. If Human2OS is something else, change the offer names and keep the structure.

## 2. Monetization patterns that repeat

1. **A free asset you get by DM.** A repo, a skill pack, a prompt list or a template, sent when someone comments a keyword. It's the most common pattern on the list (Jai, olaconleyy, the ADHD skill, nocode.joshua, Vaibhav's community). The asset is what turns a viewer into a lead.
2. **Show the result, then sell it as a service.** Apple-style sites, AI photo shoots, the LinkedIn profile makeover. The reel is the portfolio, and the service is the same thing done for the client.
3. **"How I" personal proof, then a done-for-you offer through DMs.** The LinkedIn reel is the clearest example. Selling happens in conversations, not on a sales page.
4. **Niche + tool.** "Claude for ADHD", "AI for small businesses", "Claude for LinkedIn". Narrow positioning beats generic "AI tips" for anyone with a small audience.
5. **Newsletter or community as the owned channel.** Vaibhav's main asset isn't Instagram; it's the 500K newsletter and the WhatsApp community. Sponsorships and courses come later, once the audience is large.
6. **Alerts about opportunities** (free credits, programs, new features). Cheap to make, they grow the audience fast, but they don't sell anything on their own.

**Don't copy:** AI-generated personas (olaconleyy). They work for reach, but they damage trust, and trust is what sells a service.

## 3. Executive summary: what you should do

Become **the person who sets up Claude-based workflows for overwhelmed solo operators and small businesses, using Human2OS as the method.**

- **Content** shows what you actually built, in the "How I" style ([pattern 3](#2-monetization-patterns-that-repeat)), filmed as short screen recordings.
- **Every post** points to one free asset sent by DM: a small Human2OS starter skill pack ([pattern 1](#2-monetization-patterns-that-repeat)).
- **The DM conversation** sells one done-with-you offer: a 2-week setup sprint ([patterns 2 and 3](#2-monetization-patterns-that-repeat)).
- **A low-ticket pack and a few affiliate links** catch people who aren't ready to buy the sprint.
- **A simple email list** is the one channel you own, from day 1.

That's one audience, one free asset, one paid offer and one secondary product. It's what an ADHD brain can keep running without dropping things.

## 4. Recommended offers

### Primary: the Human2OS Setup Sprint (done-with-you)

- **What:** In 2 weeks, you set up 3 Claude workflows (skills, projects or automations) around the client's real recurring tasks, such as content, client emails or weekly planning.
- **Format:** 1 × 60-min kickoff call, async building, 1 × 45-min handover call, and a short Loom video for each workflow.
- **Who it's for:** solo operators, freelancers and small business owners who know AI could help but haven't set anything up. People with ADHD who need structure are the sharpest sub-niche.
- **Price (starting point to test):** start low enough to get 3 case studies quickly, then raise the price after each 2 sold. Pick a number you're comfortable saying out loud; the first goal is case studies, not revenue.
- **Why this one:** it uses skills you've already shown, as a PM between client and developer (Innoventix) and in client work at WPHostee. It's fixed-scope, so there's no endless retainer, and every sprint becomes a "How I" post.

### Secondary: Human2OS Starter Pack (low-ticket) + affiliate

- **What:** a paid pack of 5–10 Claude skills and prompts with a short setup video. The free DM version is 2–3 of them.
- **Affiliate:** add links only to tools you really use in the sprint (for example a note-taking app, an automation tool or a screen recorder) on a single "My stack" page. Never make a post just to push a link.
- **Drop-servicing (optional, later):** if sprint clients ask for a website, pass the work on to your WPHostee network and keep a margin. Don't promote this; just say yes when asked.

## 5. Content system

### Pillars (4)

| Pillar | Example post | Pattern behind it |
|---|---|---|
| **How I built it** | "How I set up a Claude skill that writes my weekly plan in 2 minutes" | "How I" proof, showing the result |
| **Human2OS for ADHD brains** | "Why my AI setup has exactly 3 workflows, not 30" | Niche + tool |
| **Client teardown** (with permission) | "Before/after: a client's inbox workflow" | Show the result, then sell it as a service |
| **Opportunity alerts** | "Anthropic just opened X — here's who qualifies" | Opportunity alerts (use sparingly, max 1 in 5 posts) |

The three content ideas already listed in `priorități.md` slot straight in: the ADHD skill vs. Human2OS comparison (pillar 2), Claude for content with a firewall (pillar 1) and the LinkedIn profile rebuild (pillar 1).

### Formats

- **Main:** a 30–45 s screen recording with a voiceover. The first sentence states the result, then 3 steps, then "Comment SETUP and I'll send you the skill."
- **Second:** a 6–8 slide carousel that repeats the same reel as steps people can save.
- **Weekly:** 1 talking-head reel, a personal story or lesson ("How I", never "How to").

### Posting cadence (ADHD-proof)

- **Batch once a week:** 90 minutes on one day to record 3 reels. Turning each into a carousel takes about 20 minutes.
- **Post 3 times a week** (for example Mon / Wed / Fri). Skipping a day is fine; skipping the batch session is not.
- **Reply to DMs every day** for 15 minutes, with a timer, at the same hour each day.
- **Keep the weekly 20-minute research session** from README.md; it ends with one thing to test.

## 7. 30-day execution plan

### Week 1: build the foundation
- Write the free pack: 3 Human2OS skills you already use, plus a one-page setup guide.
- Set up the Instagram bio, the LinkedIn banner, headline and Featured link, and the DM keyword auto-reply.
- Write a one-page sprint offer (a Google Doc is enough): who it's for, what you set up, timeline and price.
- Record and post the first 3 reels (pillar 1).

### Week 2: start conversations
- Post 3 reels and 2 LinkedIn posts. One of them is the ADHD skill vs. Human2OS comparison.
- Send 10 personal DMs (no pitch) to people who engaged, following the method in README.md.
- Offer 2 founding-client spots at the starter price, in exchange for a case study.

### Week 3: deliver and document
- Run the first sprint. Screen-record as you build; that becomes next week's content.
- Post 3 reels, including the first client teardown (with permission).
- Assemble the paid Starter Pack from what you built in sprint 1, and add the "My stack" affiliate page.

### Week 4: make the offer public
- Post the case study as a "How I" reel and a LinkedIn post, with real numbers only.
- Open the sprint publicly at a slightly higher price.
- Review: which reel got the most DMs? Make 3 more like it next month and drop the weakest pillar.
- Update `conturi.csv`: remove the accounts that gave you nothing this month.

## 8. What to ignore / not do

- **No AI-generated persona** and no automation that might get your account banned (mass DMs, follow/unfollow bots).
- **No course and no community yet.** Those work for Vaibhav at 600K+ followers, not at the start. Revisit after 10 sprint clients.
- **No retainers.** Fixed-scope sprints only, so you never manage ongoing operations alone.
- **No more than one paid offer and one low-ticket product.** New offer ideas go in a "later" list.
- **Don't post every new AI tool.** News without a "How I" angle grows followers but doesn't bring clients.
- **No unverified income claims,** yours or copied ones (e.g. the "$10K / $400K a month" reel figures).
- **Don't promote drop-servicing or affiliate links.** They're side income that only comes up when a client asks.
- **Don't watch accounts daily.** One 20-minute session a week, as in README.md.
