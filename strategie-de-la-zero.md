# Strategy From Zero

A brand-new business, built only from what the 18 accounts in `conturi.csv` (and their grouping in `priorități.md`) show. There is no earlier project, product or audience behind it.

**How solid the research is.** I searched the web for 9 of the 18 accounts. Only one (@vaibhavsisinty) has public data on how it makes money. For the other 8, nothing about the account itself came up, and Instagram, TikTok and Facebook content can't be read from the web. So most of the conclusions below come from what each account posts about (the "Întrebarea asociată" column in `conturi.csv`), grouped by account type. Treat the patterns as signals and the prices as guesses to test, not facts.

**The business in one line:** *We set up AI for small businesses (an AI-built website plus 3 working AI workflows) as fixed-price packages, and we sell them through short videos that show the real result.*

Brand name, domain and handles are open; `[Brand]` is used as a placeholder below.

## 2. The monetization model

It's a ladder: each step brings in money on its own and leads people to the next step.

| Step | What | Price (to test) | Comes from |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Free kit** | 5 copy-paste AI workflows for small businesses (prompts + short videos), sent by DM after a comment, in exchange for an email | Free | Free-asset accounts |
| **1. Template pack** | 20–30 workflows by trade (salon, restaurant, shop, consultant) + a setup video | €29–49 | Free-asset accounts |
| **2. Main offer: "AI Launch in 7 Days"** | A one-page website built with AI + 3 working AI workflows (customer replies, social posts, offers/quotes) + a 30-min handover call | €490–990 fixed price | Accounts that show the result + personal proof |
| **3. Add-on: AI product photos** | 20 product images made with AI for the site and social media | €150–300 | Account that replaced photo shoots |
| **4. Monthly care (optional)** | Small site and workflow updates, 1 call a month | €99–199/month | B2B agency |
| **Drop-servicing** | Hand off work you don't do yourself (SEO, ads, complex sites) to vetted freelancers; you manage the client and keep a 30–50% margin | Per project | B2B agency |
| **Affiliate** | Only for tools used in the packages (website builder, hosting, AI tools, scheduler), on a single "Stack" page | Commission | Large AI educator + free-asset accounts |

**Order of focus:** step 2 first (it brings in the money), steps 0 and 1 to feed it, and everything else only once clients ask for it.

## 3. The website

**What we build:** one fast site with 4 pages. It's built with an AI site builder or a template; that is itself proof the offer works.

| Page | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Headline: "Your business, set up with AI in 7 days." Then: before/after examples, the 3 packages with prices, how it works in 3 steps, FAQ and a "Book a 15-min call" button |
| **Free kit** | One email field: "Get 5 AI workflows for your business." This is where the reels send people |
| **Work** | Case studies: before → what we did → result, with real numbers only. Starts with 1–2 sites we build for ourselves or for free |
| **Stack** | The tools we use, with affiliate links. Honest, and no reviews of tools we haven't used |

**What it sells:** the AI Launch package (main), the template pack (checkout through Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy or Stripe) and the photo add-on.

**How it looks:** clean and minimal, lots of white space, one accent colour, real screenshots from client work instead of stock images. It should load fast on a phone, since most visitors come from reels.

**Simple stack:** a site builder (Framer, Webflow or WordPress with a fast theme), a booking tool (Cal.com or Calendly), an email tool (a free plan of any newsletter tool) and a payment checkout.

## 4. Social media from zero

| Platform | Role | Account | Rhythm |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Main channel: reels, carousels, DM sales | Business account, link to the free kit, auto-reply to the keyword "KIT" | 4 reels + 2 carousels / week |
| **TikTok** | Reach (the same reels) | Business account | Same videos, same days |
| **YouTube Shorts** | Search and longer life for the same reels | New channel | Same videos |
| **Facebook** | Skip for now (only watching the accounts on the list) | — | — |

**Formats (all taken from the accounts):**

1. **Before / after** (accounts that show the result): "This salon had no website. 7 days later:" plus a screen recording, 20–40 s.
2. **Workflow demo** (free-asset accounts): one AI workflow from start to finish, ending with "Comment KIT for 5 like this."
3. **Do / Don't for business owners** (B2B agency): a talking head or carousel, "3 AI mistakes small businesses make."
4. **How I** (personal proof): the story of a real project, in 4 steps.
5. **Opportunity alert** (opportunity accounts): a new feature or program and what it means for a small business. Max 1 in 5 posts.

**Rhythm:** record once a week (2 hours = 6 videos), edit the following day, schedule the posts. Spend 20 minutes a day replying to comments and DMs. Once a week, a 20-minute session watching the accounts on the list, ending with one thing to test.

## 5. Community or LinkedIn?

**LinkedIn: yes, from week 2.** Business owners and decision-makers are there, and the personal-proof account shows the method works:

- **Profile = landing page:** a banner with one sentence ("I set up AI for small businesses in 7 days"), a headline with one result, and one link in Featured (the AI Launch package).
- **2 text posts a week,** written from the same ideas as the reels.
- **DMs:** write a short personal message to everyone who reacts or views your profile, with no pitch; offer only when they ask.

**Community: not yet.** It only works with an audience already in place (the large AI educator has 600K+). Instead:

- **Months 1–3:** an email list plus a short weekly newsletter (one workflow + one case).
- **After about 500 emails or about 10 clients:** a free group (WhatsApp, Telegram or Skool) for clients and buyers of the template pack, which is where requests for monthly care will come from.
- **A paid community** only if the free group is clearly active and people ask for more.

## 6. The 30-day plan

### Week 1: setting up
- Pick a name and check that the domain and the Instagram, TikTok and YouTube handles are free; create all the accounts.
- Build the free kit: 5 workflows (customer replies, a weekly social post, an offer/quote, product descriptions, review replies).
- Build the 4-page site; the free kit page and the email signup must be working.
- Build one demo website for an imaginary business; it's our first case study.

### Week 2: starting to post
- Record the first 6 videos (2 before/after, 2 workflow demos, 1 Do/Don't, 1 How I) and post 4 of them this week.
- Turn on the "KIT" auto-reply on Instagram.
- Set up the LinkedIn profile as a landing page; first 2 posts.
- Make a list of 30 local small businesses with a weak site or none at all (a salon, a restaurant, shops).

### Week 3: first clients
- Offer 3 "founding client" spots at a reduced price, in exchange for a case study and a short testimonial video.
- Send 10 personal messages a day (Instagram DM, LinkedIn, email) to the businesses on the list and to people who asked for the kit. Show the demo and don't pitch in the first message.
- Keep posting: 4 reels + 2 carousels + 2 LinkedIn posts.
- Find 1–2 freelancers (SEO, ads) for drop-servicing; agree on prices before you need them.

### Week 4: delivery and the first products
- Deliver the first AI Launch package; record the whole process, since it's next month's content.
- Put the template pack on sale, built from what you made for the first clients.
- Add the "Stack" page with affiliate links.
- Review: which video brought the most "KIT" comments and the most calls? Next month, make 3 like it and drop the weakest format.
- Update `conturi.csv`: add accounts that inspired something, remove the ones that brought nothing.

**Targets for day 30:** at least 100 emails, 10 discovery calls, 2–3 clients for the AI Launch package, and the template pack live. These are working targets, not a forecast.

## What we don't do

- **No AI-generated persona,** and no mass DMs or bots that could get the account banned.
- **No income claims** we can't prove.
- **No course and no paid community** in the first 3 months.
- **No more than 1 main package** until it has sold at least 5 times.
- **No posts about every new AI tool** that don't connect to a business owner's problem.
