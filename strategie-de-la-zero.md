## 1. What we take from each account type

The 18 accounts fall into 7 types.

### Type A: AI education creator at scale
**Accounts:** vaibhavsisinty (verified), Filip Gabriel AI, romanai.world
- **What they do:** short-form "here's the new AI thing" news, fast and frequent, aimed at a broad audience.
- **What they monetize (verified for Vaibhav):** a **free hub/community** for discovery, then **paid cohorts and workshops** through his company, GrowthSchool, sold via webinars. Brand deals with AI tools. He reportedly validated his first course with a **$10 pre-payment link** before building it.
- **What works:** high volume, novelty, and a free entry point that collects emails.
- **What doesn't:** public reviews of the workshops are mixed. People complain about promised content, delays and slow support. Scale without delivery quality burns trust.
- **What we take:** **pre-sell before you build** (a cheap paid link to validate demand), a **free resource hub as the lead magnet**, and a **live workshop as the bridge** to the higher-priced offer. We don't take mass volume or overpromising.
- **From romanai.world specifically:** they post "programs/perks you can claim". The **Claude for Startups** program (reported Oct 2026: 1 year of Claude Team, $1,000 in API credits, partner offers; eligibility details vary by source, so check claude.com/programs/startups) is good content, and it lowers our own tool costs. **Apply in week 1.**

### Type B: Claude skills / tooling explainers
**Accounts:** nocode.joshua (Mods in Claude Code), Jai Arora Vuyyuru (LinkedIn agent skills repo), olaconleyy ("Instagram agent skills stack"), the Claude skill account (niche skill pack), "Claude for content" (content "firewall")
- **What they do:** "here's a pack of N skills that does X", screen recordings, a GitHub repo as proof.
- **What they monetize:** mostly attention. The repos are free; the payoff is followers, newsletter subs and consulting leads. Some sell premium packs or templates.
- **What works:** **a concrete number + a specific outcome** ("11 skills that run your LinkedIn"), a **free GitHub repo** as proof of competence, and a **screen-recorded demo** that ends with a working result.
- **What doesn't:** skill packs are a commodity. There are thousands of public repos tagged `agent-skills`. Automating posting, DMs or engagement on Instagram/LinkedIn breaks platform rules and risks a **ban**. That's your own question about olaconleyy, and the answer is: automate **drafting and analysis**, never **posting, liking, following or DMing**.
- **What we take:** **open-source one small skill pack per month** as a lead magnet. Publish **"the firewall" as a principle**: AI drafts, a human approves and posts. Sell the **setup**, not the files.

### Type C: AI-for-SMB agency
**Account:** FairClick Agency (@fairclick.agency, TikTok, 303 followers on 8 Oct 2026)
- **What they do:** AI implementation for small and medium businesses, with dos and don'ts content.
- **What they monetize:** services, i.e. implementation projects.
- **What works:** "what to do / what NOT to do" is an authority format that builds trust with business owners.
- **What doesn't:** 303 followers. Generic "AI for your business" without a specific outcome doesn't scale on TikTok.
- **What we take:** the **dos/don'ts format** and the **pilot model**: a 14–30 day pilot with KPIs, permissions and approvals, aimed at a task that repeats 20–50 times a week. We sell **one specific outcome** per offer, not "AI implementation".

### Type D: "Build X with Claude" showcases
**Accounts:** "Apple-style sites" (Claude Code / Cowork skill), "AI photoshoots with Claude" (TikTok)
- **What they do:** a before/after transformation. A boring site becomes an Apple-like site; product photos become a studio shoot without a studio.
- **What works:** **visual before/after** is the strongest hook in the whole list, and the result is easy to understand in 3 seconds.
- **What we take:** two **productized services** with a fixed scope and price (see section 2), and before/after as our #1 content format.

### Type E: LinkedIn growth / monetization coach
**Account:** "Linked in mineyyy" (+ the "first $10K month from LinkedIn" reel referenced in the README; its figures are unverified)
- **The playbook (from the README notes):** profile = landing page (banner with one sentence about who you help, headline with one result); **Featured = one link to the done-for-you offer**; every post solves one pain for one person, shown as before → what I did → result; DM everyone who likes, comments or views your profile, with **no pitch**, and sell only when they ask.
- **What we take:** the whole playbook, plus the rule **"How I", not "How to"**.

### Type F: Personal-brand / app creators (format study)
**Accounts:** baiatu.cu.aplicatii, shiftsbybianca, Ioana, influencer "dots", 2× "Cont nenotat" (FB/IG)
- **Not verifiable** (not indexed). Their questions in the CSV are blank ("De completat").
- **Provisional takeaway:** for each, fill in the README card (niche, hook, length, repeating theme, CTA) **before** borrowing. "baiatu.cu.aplicatii" looks like a Romanian app-review format ("an app a day"), which suits a recurring **"tool of the week"** series. The "dots" influencer has a **signature named framework**, so we create our own named framework (see section 4).

### Type G: AI-generated persona
**Account:** olaconleyy (AI-generated profile)
- **What we take:** the lesson, not the tactic. AI personas can grow, but platforms increasingly label or limit them, and they can't sell trust-based services. **We stay a real face.** We use AI behind the scenes, as Type D does.

## 2. The resulting monetization model

A value ladder. Every rung feeds the next.

| Rung | Offer | Price (EUR, starting point) | Source type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Free | Open-source skill pack on GitHub + "AI Starter Hub" page (prompts, checklists) in exchange for an email | 0 | A, B |
| 1. Low-ticket | **Template/skill packs** (site skill, content-engine skill, LinkedIn skill), Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy | 19–49 | B |
| 2. Workshop | **Live 90-min workshop** "Build your site/content engine with Claude in one afternoon", recorded and resold | 29–79 | A |
| 3. Productized services (core revenue) | **(a) AI Website Sprint**: Apple-style landing page, 5 business days | 900–1,900 | D |
| | **(b) AI Product Photoshoot**: 20 images from client photos | 250–450 | D |
| | **(c) LinkedIn Engine Setup**: profile rewrite + 30 drafted posts + human-approved workflow | 600–1,200 | E, B |
| | **(d) 30-day AI Pilot for SMEs**: one repeating task automated, with KPIs and an approvals step | 1,500–3,500 | C |
| 4. Recurring | **Care plan**: hosting + updates + monthly content refresh / monthly pilot maintenance | 99–399/month | C, D |
| 5. Affiliate (side income, always disclosed) | Hosting, domain, design/video tools, automation tools you actually use in delivery | commission | A |
| 6. Drop-servicing (only after rung 3 has 3 delivered clients) | Resell adjacent work (video editing, copywriting in other languages, SEO audits) to vetted freelancers at a margin. **You own QA and the client relationship.** | 30–50% margin | — |

**Rules**
- **Pre-sell before building.** Each new product gets a payment or waitlist link first. Build it only after 10 payments (or 3 for services).
- **One flagship.** For 30 days, the **AI Website Sprint** is the hero offer. It's the most visual, the easiest to prove and the easiest to upsell into a care plan.
- **Revenue target for month 1 (realistic):** 2 sprints + 1 photoshoot + 15–30 template sales ≈ **€2,000–4,500**. This is a target, not a promise; adjust it after week 2.
- **Costs:** a Claude plan (or Startup Program credits), a domain + hosting, Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy, Cal.com, an email tool on a free tier. Under €60/month at the start.

## 3. The website: what we build, what it sells, how it looks

**Built with our own method.** The site is itself the first Apple-style showcase, built with Claude Code. It's proof.

**Structure (one domain, 6 pages max)**
1. **Home**: hero with one sentence ("AI websites and content systems for small businesses, delivered in 5 days."), one before/after slider, 3 offers, 3 case studies, one CTA: **Book a 20-min call**.
2. **Services**: the 4 productized services with fixed scope, fixed price, timeline and what's NOT included.
3. **Work**: before/after case studies in the format *before → what I did → result (real numbers only)*.
4. **Shop**: templates and skill packs (links to Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy).
5. **Free Hub**: free resources + GitHub link, with email capture.
6. **Workshop**: next live date + replay purchase.

**Look and feel**
- Apple-inspired: lots of whitespace, one sans-serif font family, big headlines, one accent color, scroll-triggered reveals, full-width product shots.
- Mobile-first. Lighthouse score 90+ is part of the pitch.
- No stock photos. Only real before/after screenshots and your own face.

**Stack:** static site (Astro or Next.js) generated with Claude Code, deployed to Vercel/Netlify, Cal.com for bookings, Stripe/Lemon Squeezy for payments, a simple email tool for the hub.

**Conversion path:** reel/post → profile link → Home → (a) book a call, or (b) free hub email → 3-email sequence → workshop → service.

## 4. Social media from zero

**Signature framework (taken from the "dots" influencer and A):** name the method, for example **"The 3-Layer Build: Draft (AI) → Check (Human) → Ship (System)."** Every piece of content refers back to it. It is also our "firewall" principle (from B).

**Accounts (start with 3, not 6)**

| Platform | Role | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Main short-form channel; before/after reels, carousels | 1 |
| **LinkedIn** (personal profile) | Sales channel for B2B services | 1 |
| **TikTok** | Reuse IG reels for reach | 2 (repost only) |
| GitHub | Proof: free skill packs | supporting |
| YouTube / Facebook | Not in month 1 | later |

**Content pillars and formats**

| Pillar | Format | Source type | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Before/After** | 20–30s reel: ugly → Apple-style in a split screen; site, photos, profile | D | 35% |
| **How I built it** | 45–60s screen recording, "How I", never "How to" | B, E | 25% |
| **Dos & Don'ts** | Carousel/reel: "5 AI mistakes SMEs make" | C | 15% |
| **Tool / perk of the week** | 30s news: a new Claude feature, startup perks, a tool | A, F | 15% |
| **Behind the business** | Revenue, client wins, real numbers only | E | 10% |

**Hook rules:** a result in the first 2 seconds, a number when it's real, a visible screen. **CTA rules:** one CTA per post: "comment SITE and I'll send the skill" (manual DMs only) or "link in bio".

**Rhythm (sustainable for one person)**
- Instagram: **4 reels + 2 carousels a week**; stories daily (3 min).
- LinkedIn: **3 posts a week** (Mon/Wed/Fri) + 15 minutes of meaningful comments a day.
- TikTok: repost the 4 reels.
- GitHub: 1 small release a month.
- **Batching:** one 3-hour production block a week (Sunday). Claude drafts scripts and captions; you record and approve (the firewall).
- **Review:** 20 minutes a week on the watchlist (README method). Each session ends with one test for the next week.

**What we never automate:** posting, liking, following, commenting, DMs. That's the ban risk, and it kills trust.

## 5. Community or LinkedIn: is it worth it, and how to start

**Verdict: LinkedIn now, community later.**

- **LinkedIn is worth it from day 1.** The buyers of €900–3,500 services (SME owners, marketing managers) are there, and the account-E playbook converts without paid ads.
  - Banner: *"I build AI websites and content systems for small businesses, in 5 days."*
  - Headline: *"AI Website Sprints for SMEs | Apple-quality sites in 5 days | Claude-built"*
  - Featured: **one link** to the Website Sprint page.
  - Posts: before → what I did → result.
  - DMs: personal messages to people who engage, with no pitch. Sell only when asked. Max 10 a day, all written by you.
- **A community is NOT worth it in month 1.** With zero audience, an empty community kills trust. Instead, the **free hub + email list** is the "community" for month 1, and the **live workshop** is the gathering.
- **When to start one:** at **300+ email subscribers OR 30+ workshop buyers**. Then open a free group (Skool free tier or Discord) where workshop buyers get access. Account A shows the model: free community → paid cohort. Later, add a paid tier (€19–29/month) with a monthly live build + template drops.
